# 📊 Amazon E-Commerce Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI analytics project designed to analyze Amazon product performance, pricing dynamics, bestseller badge impacts, and customer ratings.

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard_preview.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
This interactive dashboard provides business insights into e-commerce product demand and revenue generation. It helps stakeholders understand pricing sweet spots, high-performing product categories, and the direct impact of promotion badges on sales volume.

## 🔑 Key Features & Insights
* **Top Revenue Generators:** Identifies the top 10 products contributing highest to overall estimated revenue.
* **Pricing Demand Distribution:** Analyzes sales volume across $10 price bins to find optimal price points ($0–$500).
* **Bestseller Badge Impact:** Compares average unit sales between badge holders and regular listings.
* **Rating vs. Sales Volume:** Evaluates customer feedback distribution against total monthly purchases.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Dynamic slicers for Bestseller status and Rating ranges.

## 🛠️ Data & Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (Data cleaning, type casting, custom binning)
* **DAX & Calculated Measures:** Custom columns for estimated revenue and aggregate KPI measures
* **Data Fields:** Price, Units Sold, Bestseller Badges, Product Titles, Ratings
